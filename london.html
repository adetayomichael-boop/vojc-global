Who we are

Voice of Jesus Christ Parish is part of Voice of Jesus Christ Global, a family of Celestial Church of Christ parishes and fellowships. Our London congregation is committed to holiness, prayer, and the move of the Holy Spirit, serving our community as a place of worship, healing, and fellowship — open to all who seek God.

Led by our resident minister and a team of dedicated elders and workers, we hold services rooted in scripture, prayer, and praise, and we welcome visitors of every background to worship with us.

"Let everything that hath breath praise the Lord." Psalm 150:6
