Who we are

Voice of Jesus Christ Global is a family of Celestial Church of Christ parishes and fellowships, each independently led and rooted in its own community, united by shared tradition, doctrine, and purpose. From London to Lagos, Coventry to Dubai to Canada, every parish holds its own services and ministry — while remaining part of one worldwide body of believers.
